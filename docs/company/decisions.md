# Decisions — Company Research

> **Layer:** 4 — Decision Support
> **Status:** Frozen

---

## Key Decisions

### D1: Must Build on Industry Research (2026-07-06)

**Decision:** Company Research must be built on Industry Research foundation.

**Rationale:** Without industry context, company analysis is fragmented. Industry framework provides the analytical lens.

### D2: Must Output Investment Recommendation (2026-07-06)

**Decision:** Company Research must output Buy/Hold/Sell recommendation with rationale.

**Rationale:** Layer 4 is Decision Support. Without actionable recommendation, it's just a company profile.

### D3: Evidence-Traceable Conclusions (2026-07-06)

**Decision:** All conclusions in Company Research must be traceable to Evidence.

**Rationale:** Investment decisions must be verifiable. Evidence chain ensures accountability.
