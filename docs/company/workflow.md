# Workflow — Company Research

> **Layer:** 4 — Decision Support
> **Status:** Concept frozen, implementation pending

---

## Research Process

```
Step 1: Company Selection
  ↓ Select company from Research Terminal Company Radar
Step 2: Industry Framework Reference
  ↓ Load relevant Industry Framework (prerequisite)
Step 3: Fundamental Analysis
  ↓ Analyze company within framework context
Step 4: Valuation
  ↓ Apply valuation methods within framework
Step 5: Risk Assessment
  ↓ Identify and assess risks
Step 6: Investment Recommendation
  ↓ Output Buy/Hold/Sell + rationale
Step 7: Evidence Chain
  ↓ Ensure all conclusions traceable to evidence
```

## Quality Gates

- Must reference Industry Framework
- Must output investment recommendation with rationale
- All conclusions must have Evidence support
- Must use unified analysis framework (DCF/EV-EBITDA/PE/PS/SOTP)
