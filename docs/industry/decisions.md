# Decisions — Industry Research

> **Layer:** 3 — Industry Framework
> **Status:** Frozen

---

## Key Decisions

### D1: Framework Not Report (2026-07-06)

**Decision:** Industry Research outputs Framework, not Report.

**Rationale:** One-time reports don't accumulate knowledge. Frameworks are reusable and evolve with research, supporting the system mission of cognitive accumulation.

### D2: Prerequisite for Company Research (2026-07-06)

**Decision:** Company Research (Layer 4) must build on Industry Research (Layer 3).

**Rationale:** Without industry framework, company analysis lacks context. Industry framework provides the analytical lens for company evaluation.

### D3: Framework Versioning (2026-07-06)

**Decision:** Each industry framework evolves through versioned iterations.

**Rationale:** Knowledge accumulation requires tracking how understanding evolves. Version control enables cognitive evolution tracking.
