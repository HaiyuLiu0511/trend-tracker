# Workflow — Monthly Outlook

> **Layer:** 5 — Knowledge Evolution
> **Status:** Concept frozen, implementation pending

---

## Monthly Generation Process

```
Step 1: Collect Monthly Research Records
  ↓ Gather all Daily Briefings, Research Terminal outputs, Industry Research, Company Research
Step 2: Cognitive Evolution Analysis
  ↓ Compare with previous month — what understanding changed?
Step 3: Investment Map Update
  ↓ Add new, remove outdated, adjust existing entries
Step 4: New Theme Identification
  ↓ Identify themes that emerged during the month
Step 5: Framework Evolution
  ↓ Update Industry Frameworks with new cognitive insights
Step 6: Feedback Generation
  ↓ Define focus areas for next cycle's Daily Briefing
Step 7: Output Generation
  ↓ Generate monthly-outlook-YYYY-MM.md + update investment-mapping.md
```

## Quality Gates

- Must compare with previous month (not just summarize)
- Must mark evolution explicitly
- Must update Investment Mapping
- Must generate feedback for Layer 1
