# Decisions — Monthly Outlook

> **Layer:** 5 — Knowledge Evolution
> **Status:** Frozen

---

## Key Decisions

### D1: Knowledge Evolution Not News Summary (2026-07-06)

**Decision:** Monthly Outlook is Knowledge Evolution, not news summary.

**Rationale:** System mission is cognitive accumulation. Monthly news summary doesn't accumulate knowledge. Cognitive evolution tracking does.

### D2: Must Compare with Previous Month (2026-07-06)

**Decision:** Monthly Outlook must compare current cognition with previous month's cognition.

**Rationale:** Evolution is only visible through comparison. Without comparison, it's just a summary.

### D3: Must Update Investment Mapping (2026-07-06)

**Decision:** Monthly Outlook must update Investment Mapping.

**Rationale:** Investment Mapping is the cognitive accumulation carrier. Without regular updates, the map becomes stale and loses value.

### D4: Feedback to Daily Briefing (2026-07-06)

**Decision:** Monthly Outlook output influences next cycle's Daily Briefing focus.

**Rationale:** Knowledge Flow is cyclical, not linear. Monthly insights should shape what's important to watch daily.
