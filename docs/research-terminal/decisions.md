# Decisions — Research Terminal

> **Layer:** 2 — Research Navigator
> **Status:** Frozen

---

## Key Decisions

### D1: Navigator Not Knowledge Base (2026-07-06)

**Decision:** Research Terminal is positioned as Navigator, not Knowledge Base.

**Rationale:** Observation Phase identified that Research Terminal was being used as a knowledge store, causing overlap with Daily Briefing and diluting its navigation purpose. Navigator positioning enforces strict layer responsibility.

**Impact:** Research Terminal must not store deep content. It only navigates to Layer 3/4.

### D2: V1 Tab Format Frozen (2026-07-06)

**Decision:** 3 Tabs frozen as the output format for Research Terminal.

**Rationale:** Dashboard provides overview, Theme Radar and Company Radar provide navigation targets. Minimal tabs enforce Navigator positioning.

### D3: Boundary with Daily Briefing (2026-07-06)

**Decision:** Research Terminal boundary must be clearly defined to prevent overlap with Daily Briefing.

**Rationale:** P0-002 identified that Research Terminal content overlapped with Daily Briefing. Clear boundary prevents duplication.
